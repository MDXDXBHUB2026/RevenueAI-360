# RevenueAI 360: Customer 360 Context & Intelligence Model

## 1. Unified Context Model

The foundational premise of RevenueAI 360 is that customer interactions rarely stay confined to a single channel. A prospect may initiate an inquiry via Email, follow up on WhatsApp, raise an emergency ticket via WebChat, and receive internal escalation notifications via Slack.

RevenueAI 360 unifies these fragmented touchpoints into a single, cohesive **Customer 360 Aggregate Record**.

## 2. Customer 360 Aggregation Service (`services/customer360.py`)

The `Customer360Service` executes an atomic aggregate query that compiles:
- **Account Metadata**: Tier, ARR, Industry, Primary Domain, Account Executive.
- **People & Contacts**: Identified stakeholders, titles, email addresses, phone numbers.
- **Active Channel Identities**: Mappings to WhatsApp handles, Slack usernames, email aliases.
- **Chronological Interactions**: Unified timeline of inbound inquiries, outbound replies, and internal notes.
- **Active Deals & Pipeline**: Active pipeline stages, expected ARR, close dates.
- **Support & Complaints**: Open tickets, SLA timers, repeat grievance counts, severity.
- **Next-Best-Action**: Dynamic recommendation generated by the NBA Engine.

## 3. The Next-Best-Action (NBA) Engine (`services/next_best_action.py`)

The NBA engine applies deterministic business rules over the Customer 360 state:

1. **Active Grievance Priority**:
   If an account has an active, unresolved complaint (severity `high` or `critical`):
   - **Recommended Action**: `RESOLVE_COMPLAINT_FIRST`
   - **Suppression Policy**: Immediately suppress marketing emails, automated drip sequences, and sales up-sell pitches.
   - **Next Step**: Conduct technical root-cause investigation, notify the executive sponsor, and confirm customer satisfaction before commercial re-engagement.

2. **Commercial Expansion**:
   If an account has an active discovery-stage deal and zero open grievances:
   - **Recommended Action**: `SCHEDULE_TECHNICAL_DISCOVERY`
   - **Next Step**: Provide tailored architecture proposals and ROI benchmarks.

3. **Routine Support Follow-up**:
   If a support case was recently resolved:
   - **Recommended Action**: `SEND_CSAT_AND_CLOSING_VERIFICATION`
   - **Next Step**: Confirm zero residual shipment disruption.
